description: |
  A mobile app for following the NBA in Spanish. It brings together live scores, full box scores, standings, player and team profiles, and an in-house estimate of the win probability of every game.

  Data comes from public sources, including the NBA's official statistics and ESPN. A Python ETL pipeline normalises it and loads it into a PostgreSQL database hosted on Supabase. Updates are automated with GitHub Actions: daily for games and predictions, every six hours for news and injuries, and weekly for teams and rosters.
